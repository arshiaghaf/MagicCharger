# Architecture

The AppKit accessory app owns independent monitoring, controls and per-device NSStatusItems. LSUIElement avoids a permanent Dock icon. Launch/reopen presents one controls window; closing it keeps the process alive.

Public IOKit registry matching/termination callbacks and interest hints trigger coalesced background snapshots. A fixed 250 ms window combines bursts without postponing the final read indefinitely. Only one collection runs at a time; one subsequent collection captures changes arriving during a read. Failed registration/enumeration remains dirty with capped exponential backoff and is retried by fallback polling even on stable topology; successful registrations are retained without duplicates. Bounded fallback polling is 30 seconds idle/unknown and 10 seconds charging. UI changes occur on the main thread. Sleep invalidates in-flight work and visible items; wake resamples. Each service is decoded from one instantaneous `IORegistryEntryCreateCFProperties` dictionary snapshot, as documented in Apple's IOKitLib API, to avoid mixing separately queried transition values. Only selected accessory keys are accessed; dictionaries and raw addresses stay transient. This cannot make a stale driver value fresh or make separate services atomic. Collection errors are distinct from a successful empty result, clear charging UI/continuity conservatively, and retry automatically. Driver freshness is not inferred from polling time.

ChargingDecoder uses tri-state evidence and narrow model/OS profiles. Conflicting duplicate observations are suppressed. Installation-keyed identity supports conservative notification continuity across reconnects. Menu icons cache the device glyph and its visibility preference; percentages and status emoji update as text without animation timers. Full takes precedence over the charging emoji at 100% only when the reading is already eligible for display; idle, unknown and stale readings remain hidden.

ChargingStarts needs a recent verified idle baseline. Every threshold also needs a fresh process-local charging baseline after launch, sleep, absence, unknown or stale evidence. Thresholds persists minimal session state and consumes historical/disabled crossings; expired history is pruned and saved on load and on every collection, including empty collections. A Boolean charging value at 100% does not consume the full milestone without supported full-state evidence. The highest enabled eligible milestone wins. UserNotifications authorization is independent of alert preferences; pending and async disabled alerts are guarded. Lifecycle tickets invalidate pending automatic submissions after interruptions, percentage regressions or preference changes, including an off/on toggle before the authorization callback returns. Authorization and submission are injected at the edge for deterministic integration checks; denial and submission failure do not replay consumed crossings. Native notification submission is not proof of visible presentation.

References: [IOKit](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/iokit), [Apple registry notification implementation](https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/IOKitUser/blob/main/ionotifyCB.c), [NSStatusBar](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appkit/nsstatusbar). Private accessory IOPowerSources APIs are intentionally unused.
